# Structural Crime Scene Processing

## Field Forms Packet

## Draft 1

---

## Purpose

This packet translates the Structural Crime Scene Processing method into ready-to-use field and review forms.

These forms are not administrative decoration.

They exist to prevent the method from collapsing back into oral explanation, hidden expert rescue, premature narrative, and unowned residue.

Each form is intentionally compact. If a form becomes too large, users will route the real logic around it. If a form is too thin, it will not change behavior.

---

## How to Use This Packet

The packet is designed for phased use.

Use:

- Form 1 during arrival and stabilization
- Forms 2-7 during scene processing
- Form 8 at the lab handoff
- Forms 9-10 during active case management
- Form 11 during post-scene review
- Form 12 as the cover sheet for the integrated scene package

Not every form must be used on every scene at full length.

But the categories they force into view should always be present in the work.
