# Structural Crime Scene Processing

## Quick Field Card

## Draft 1

---

## Core Rule

Do not ask "What happened here?" first.

Ask:

- What must be preserved before meaning drifts?
- What is the current state?
- What is the next expected transition?
- What anchors outrank the story forming in our heads?

---

## Arrival

### Establish four boundaries

- Physical
- Informational
- Authority
- Interpretive

### Protect diagnosability

- Capture life-safety impacts on the scene
- Start access logging
- Stop uncontrolled narrative spread
- Preserve device and environmental states when feasible
- Record early impressions as provisional anchors, not conclusions

### First questions

- What is perishable right now?
- What is already changing?
- Who is already acting as a carrier of scene meaning?
- Which boundary is only visible, not enforced?

- Next expected transition:
- Feasible window:
- What would count as stall:

Quiet is not closure.

Noise is not progress.

---

## Anchor Check

Prioritize:

1. Instrumented discontinuities
2. Access and timing bounds
3. Device and environmental state changes
4. Provisional human bounds

Ask:

- What does this anchor constrain?
- What does it not constrain?
- Which anchor conflict matters most?

---

## Graph Check

Before story, map:

- people
- places
- objects / traces
- devices
- interfaces
- times
- unknowns

Do not overfill the graph with guessed relations.

An honest unknown is stronger than a smooth fiction.

---

## Collection Check

Do not collect by habit alone.

Rank by:

1. Perishability
2. Hypothesis-splitting value
3. Contamination sensitivity
4. Propagation-path value
5. Contextual breadth

For each collection move, ask:

- What live question does this serve?
- Which hypotheses does it split?
- What distinction could be destroyed if I collect or package this badly?

---

## Lab Check

The lab is not a confirmation service.

Send:

- the question
- the top live hypotheses
- the relevant anchors
- the test order if order matters
- what a null would mean
- what a null would not mean

---

## Reconstruction Check

Reconstruction must:

- phase-align to anchors
- respect the scene graph
- preserve lawful eliminations
- state what remains unresolved

Separate:

- observed state
- inferred sequence
- conditional interpretation

---

## Closure Check

Before transfer or release, state:

- Closure criterion
- Resolved questions
- Unresolved questions
- Live inhibitors
- Owned deferrals
- Reentry conditions

Administrative release is not analytical closure.

---

## Drift Check

If any of the following are true, the old model is returning:

- the case only makes sense when explained aloud
- a calm file is being treated as a closed file
- evidence volume is replacing ranking discipline
- a familiar category is outrunning the feature pass
- hidden experts are rescuing weak packages
- throughput is outranking uncertainty reduction

---

## Final Reminder

The goal is not to sound certain.

The goal is to become structurally honest enough that certainty appears only where it has been earned.
