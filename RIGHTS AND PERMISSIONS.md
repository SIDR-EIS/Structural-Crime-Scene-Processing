# Rights and Permissions

Structural Crime Scene Processing is publicly shared, but it is not open source.

Unless a separate written permission states otherwise, all original text and original project materials in this repository are made available on an `all rights reserved` basis, with the limited educational permissions described below.

## Core Rule

Treat this repository the way you would treat a published book that is being made publicly accessible for reading, study, citation, and legitimate instructional use.

Public access does not mean unrestricted reuse.

## What Is Permitted Without Asking First

The following uses are permitted without prior written permission:

- reading, downloading, and studying the material
- citing the material in scholarship, journalism, policy work, teaching, or professional discussion using normal attribution and citation standards
- quoting limited portions in the ordinary way that published works are quoted for commentary, review, analysis, or discussion
- duplicating or distributing the material for noncommercial educational use, classroom use, workshop use, or internal training use, provided that attribution and citation are preserved and no resale occurs
- sharing links to the repository or to individual documents for reading, discussion, or instruction

## Conditions on Educational Duplication

Educational duplication is permitted only if all of the following are true:

1. the use is genuinely educational, instructional, research-oriented, or internal to training
2. the material is not sold, relicensed, or repackaged for sale
3. standard attribution and citation are provided
4. authorship, title, and repository identification are not removed
5. rights notices are kept intact
6. the material is not presented as if it were authored by someone else

## What Is Not Permitted Without Prior Written Permission

The following uses are not permitted without prior written permission from the rights holder:

- resale of the material in whole or in part
- packaging the material into another book, manual, workbook, course pack, consulting package, paid training product, or other derivative publication
- republishing substantial portions as part of another work or branded product
- adapting, rewriting, translating, or remixing the work for redistribution as a new product or publication
- commercial redistribution, licensing, sublicensing, or platform resale
- removing attribution, citation expectations, or rights notices
- presenting the work as public-domain or open-source material

## Attribution and Citation Expectations

If you quote, teach from, distribute for educational purposes, or cite this repository, use normal professional attribution and citation standards.

At minimum, preserve:

- the project title: `Structural Crime Scene Processing`
- the document title you are using
- the repository or source location
- the author or publisher identification used by the repository owner

If you are using excerpts in a syllabus, slide deck, handout, workshop packet, or institutional training memo, include enough citation information that a reader can find the original source and understand that the material remains rights-reserved.

## Public Repository Note

This repository is intended to be publicly viewable so the work can circulate and be used responsibly.

That public visibility does not waive rights, does not convert the project into open-source material, and does not authorize resale or repackaging into other works.

## Permissions Requests

If someone wants to:

then permission should be requested from the repository owner before use.
