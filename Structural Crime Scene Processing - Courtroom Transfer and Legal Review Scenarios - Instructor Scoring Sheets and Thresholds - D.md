## Academy Evaluation Principles for Legal Scenario Work

### Principle 1: Score bounded legal structure, not persuasive fluency

A cleaner affidavit, memo, or demonstrative should score lower if it outruns the package.

### Principle 2: Do not punish residue that remains visible lawfully

Legal-stage learners should not lose points merely because they refuse to counterfeit certainty.

### Principle 3: Penalize rhetorical closure heavily

The most dangerous downstream failure is overclaim that sounds professionally mature.

### Principle 4: Distinguish structure weakness from urgency collapse

Some learners understand the method in discussion but abandon it when charging, warrant, or trial pressure appears. That requires a different remediation path than simple conceptual confusion.

### Principle 5: Require transferability and rewrite discipline

If the legal output cannot survive transfer without oral rescue, or if the learner cannot rewrite overclaimed language into bounded language, the learner is not ready for independent downstream use.

### Automatic flags

Check any that apply:

- Contemplated legal move treated as self-validating
- Observation, bounded sequence, and rhetoric collapsed into one layer
- Actor, motive, or intent language outruns anchors
- Residue or reentry condition deleted for cleanliness
- Oral rescue treated as normal transfer method

If any automatic flag is present at a severe level, the learner should not receive an unqualified pass even with a numerically acceptable total.

## Fast Threshold Table

### Score bands

`20-24`

Pass, assuming no severe automatic flag.

`16-19`

Pass with targeted follow-up if no severe automatic flag is present.

`12-15`

Remediate and re-run.

`0-11`

Not ready. Return to foundational downstream-legal retraining before re-assessment.

### Severe automatic flags that override score

Any of the following should override a simple pass:

- learner states actor action, motive, intent, or exact sequence as settled without lawful anchor basis
- learner hides meaningful residue or ignores a live reentry condition because it makes the legal product less clean
- learner accepts a package or demonstrative that only works through hidden oral rescue
- learner uses a warrant, charging memo, or trial visual to rewrite the package rather than reflect it
- learner responds to structural weakness by demanding more confidence instead of more structure

These are not minor polish issues.

They indicate the learner is still structurally unsafe under downstream legal pressure.

## Pass / Remediate Decision Matrix

### Pass

Use when:

- total score is 20 or higher
- no severe override flag is present
- output is transferable
- legal framing remains within bounded support

### Pass with targeted follow-up

Use when:

- total score is 16-19
- the learner is structurally sound overall
- the weaknesses are bounded to one or two dimensions
- the learner does not create dangerous rhetorical closure

Follow-up should be assigned within the next training cycle.

### Remediate and re-run

Use when:

- total score is 12-15
- or one severe automatic flag appears
- or the learner understands the method in discussion but collapses under charging, warrant, transfer, or trial pressure

Re-run should occur after targeted remediation rather than simple repetition.

### Not yet ready for independent downstream legal use

Use when:

- total score is 11 or below
- or multiple severe automatic flags appear
- or the learner repeatedly converts structural limits into polished legal overclaim

These learners need a reset, not a confidence boost.

## Remediation Routing Guide

Use the learner's weakest dimensions to assign remediation.

### Weak in legal-move matching discipline

Symptoms:

- assumes the legal move is justified because the file has reached legal review
- treats urgency as sufficiency
- cannot distinguish full, partial, and not-yet support

Assign:

- move-matching drills
- threshold-comparison exercises
- side-by-side review of overreached and properly matched legal moves

### Weak in structure-versus-rhetoric separation

Symptoms:

- blends observation, bounded sequence, conditional interpretation, and rhetoric
- cannot identify where the package ends and the memo begins
- treats smooth summaries as evidence

Assign:

- four-layer separation drills
- affidavit disassembly exercises
- summary-to-package comparison drills

### Weak in anchor and elimination discipline

Symptoms:

- summary language outranks anchors
- learner cannot say what anchors do not prove
- elimination logic is absent or hand-waved

Assign:

- anchor-ranking exercises for legal readers
- "what this anchor does not prove" rewrite drills
- elimination-table review exercises

### Weak in overclaim and grammar-inflation detection

Symptoms:

- aggressive verbs pass without challenge
- motive or intent language appears unsupported
- hidden conditionals are flattened into declarative fact

Assign:

- verb-audit drills
- grammar-inflation markups
- affidavit and memo rewrite exercises

### Weak in residue and reentry honesty

Symptoms:

- residue treated as embarrassing clutter
- reentry resisted because posture already exists
- unresolveds erased to make the legal product cleaner

Assign:

- residue-tolerance review drills
- reentry-trigger case reviews
- bounded-theory narrowing exercises

### Weak in transferability and handoff review

Symptoms:

- accepts oral rescue as normal
- package maturity judged by prior confidence instead of explicit structure
- hidden experts go unnoticed

Assign:

- blind-transfer legal review drills
- transfer-fragility audits
- hidden-reconciliation identification workshops

### Weak in bounded rewrite quality

Symptoms:

- rewrite remains overclaimed
- rewrite becomes so vague it stops being useful
- learner cannot preserve utility while narrowing support

Assign:

- bounded-paragraph rewrite drills
- before-and-after legal language comparison exercises
- memo and demonstrative narrowing drills

### Weak in structure-request and redesign quality

Symptoms:

- asks for a cleaner story rather than better structure
- follow-up requests are broad, dramatic, or unsupported
- no redesign signal goes back upstream

Assign:

- structure-request drafting drills
- highest-value follow-up selection exercises
- downstream-to-upstream redesign memo practice

## Legal Academy Pass / Remediate Thresholds

Use these thresholds only after at least:

- two downstream legal scenarios
- one timed legal-review drill
- one bounded rewrite or transferability check

### Ready for independent downstream legal use

Requirements:

- no severe override flag in the most recent two assessments
- average score of 20 or higher
- no dimension below 2 in the latest final assessment
- successful transferability and rewrite check

### Ready with supervision

Requirements:

- average score of 16 or higher
- no more than two dimensions below 2
- no repeated severe rhetorical-closure pattern
- supervising attorney or training lead aware of weak dimensions

### Needs targeted remediation

Requirements:

- average score between 12 and 15
- or repeated weakness concentrated in one to three dimensions
- or pressure performance materially below classroom performance

### Needs foundational retraining

Requirements:

- average score below 12
- or repeated severe override flags
- or persistent inability to distinguish package truth from legal storytelling

### Promotion rule

No learner should move to independent downstream legal use merely because the average is high if severe overclaim or residue-erasure behavior remains present.

The method is designed to protect against elegant legal distortion, not to reward it.

## Recommended Instructor Workflow

For each learner:

1. score the exercise using the Master Legal Scenario Scoring Sheet
2. complete the Legal Exercise-Specific Addendum Sheet
3. assign a threshold status
4. route remediation by weakest dimension
5. record cohort status
6. re-run under legal pressure after remediation

This keeps evaluation from becoming anecdotal or charisma-driven.

## Common Instructor Failure Modes

### Passing fluent legal writers too early

If the learner sounds courtroom-ready but quietly outruns anchors, the instructor is grading polish instead of structure.

### Using the total score without override judgment

The number matters, but severe rhetorical closure must override the number.

### Treating slower bounded writing as weakness

In early training, careful lawful narrowing is usually preferable to fast confident overclaim.

### Remediating by asking for a "cleaner memo"

That usually reinforces the exact failure pattern the training is trying to correct.

### Accepting oral rescue because the learner is experienced

If the package still depends on live explanation, experience has become a substitute for transferability.
